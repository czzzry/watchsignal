# WatchSignal learned recommendation artifacts

These files are derived from the MovieLens 32M research dataset and are used by the private WatchSignal pilot.

MovieLens is provided by GroupLens Research at the University of Minnesota under its research-use license.
The dataset and transformations must not be used for commercial or revenue-bearing purposes without permission from GroupLens.

Source and citation: <https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/32m/> and Harper and Konstan, 2015.

The catalog and link map contain movie identifiers and metadata only.
They do not contain household profiles, Taste Lab entries, or private reactions.
